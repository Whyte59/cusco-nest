What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in starts at 14:00 and check-out is until 11:00. Reception is open 24 hours to support your arrival and departure.

Are Wi-Fi and parking free?

Yes. Wi-Fi and parking are free for guests — you stay connected and don't have to worry about finding a space.

Is there an airport transfer?

Yes, Cusco Nest offers an airport transfer service. Arrange the details directly with the property via WhatsApp before arrival.

Do the apartments have a kitchen?

Yes, the apartments have a fully equipped kitchen, ideal for preparing meals calmly and saving during your stay.

Does the property welcome families?

Yes. There is a family room and a three-bedroom apartment, plus a kitchen and living spaces — comfortable options for families and groups.

How does cancellation work?

Selected rates include free cancellation. Conditions vary by offer and date; confirm the policy when booking.

How do I book?

You can talk directly to the property via WhatsApp or compare availability and prices on Agoda. Support is personal, from check-in to the transfer.
